Yes. Every listing Express Briefs generates for you is yours to use commercially, with no attribution or licensing required. Since it's AI-generated, we recommend a quick read-through for accuracy before you publish.

Yes — Express Briefs is built for a global audience, with pricing and payment options that work internationally, not just for US-based agents.
