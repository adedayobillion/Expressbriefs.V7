Why TikTok needs its own format

TikTok has almost no tolerance for a slow start — you have roughly three seconds to earn a full watch before someone scrolls past. A script written like a listing description will lose the viewer immediately; a script written like a hook keeps them through the walkthrough.

Tips for a TikTok listing that performs

  • Open with a question or bold claim, not the address — “Would you believe this is under $400K?” beats “Welcome to this 3-bed home.”
  • Write in short spoken sentences, not written prose — it needs to sound natural read aloud over a walkthrough.
  • Save one standout feature for a mid-video reveal (“but wait until you see the backyard”) to keep people watching past the halfway point.
  • End with a direct, simple action: “Comment SOLD if you want the address” drives far more engagement than a generic “contact me.”
