Why Instagram needs its own format

Instagram is a scroll-stopping medium first, a sales channel second. If the first line doesn't earn a pause, the rest of the caption never gets read — no matter how good the stay / package is.

Tips for a Instagram post that performs

  • Open with a hook, not a headline — a bold claim, a number, or a question, not "Stay / Package available."
  • Use line breaks generously; a wall of text reads as an ad and gets scrolled past.
  • Close with 20-30 hashtags mixing location/niche tags with broader discovery tags — this is what actually drives non-follower reach.
  • Still include the essentials (price, key details) — hook gets the pause, details get the DM.
